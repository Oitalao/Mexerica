From dragging to done

  1. foto2.jpg Space

    Drag

    Drag one or more files and press Space while holding the mouse button.

  2. CtrlAltShift

    Choose a wheel

    Press Space again, or use Shift, Ctrl and Alt to switch between Convert, Tools and Actions.

  3. foto2.jpg

    Drop

    Drop the file on an option, or use the arrow keys and Enter.

  4. foto2.jpgoriginal, untouchedfoto2.webpconverted copy

    Done

    The result is saved next to the original, which stays unchanged.

The wheel adapts to your file

Press Ctrl with the wheel open: photo tools on one side, video tools on the other.

From photos to spreadsheets

  • HEICJPG

    Images

    JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, camera RAW, PSD and more.

  • MOVMP4

    Video

    MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and animated GIF.

  • WAVMP3

    Audio

    MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus and AIFF.

  • DOCXPDF

    Documents and slides

    Word, PowerPoint, HWP, ODT and RTF.

  • PDFDOCX

    PDF

    To Word, Excel, PowerPoint and images, and back.

  • RARZIP

    Archives

    ZIP, 7Z and TAR; also opens RAR and ISO.

  • XLSXCSV

    Spreadsheets

    XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV and PDF.

  • MDPDF

    Text and e-books

    Markdown, TXT, HTML, EPUB, DOCX and PDF.

  • SRTVTT

    Subtitles

    SRT, VTT and TXT.
